Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who this applies to

Affiliator is a personal, single-user tool run by its owner on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other person.

Data the tool handles

  • From TikTok (with the owner's consent): the owner's TikTok open ID and display name (scope user.info.basic), and OAuth tokens needed to call the API. Tokens are stored in the operating system's credential store on the owner's computer.
  • Videos the owner chooses: video files and captions are stored locally on the owner's computer and are sent to TikTok only when the owner schedules an upload to their own TikTok drafts (scope video.upload).
  • Local records: a local database stores clip names, schedules, and upload results.

How data is used

Only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account as drafts, and to show the owner the status of those uploads.

Sharing and selling

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used for analytics or profiling. The only external service contacted is TikTok's API.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) and can delete the stored token and local files at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the owner through the profile of the repository that hosts this page.

Kebijakan Privasi (ringkasan dalam Bahasa Indonesia)

Affiliator adalah alat pribadi untuk satu pengguna yang berjalan di komputer pemiliknya, bukan layanan publik, dan tidak mengumpulkan data orang lain. Data yang ditangani: ID dan nama tampilan TikTok pemilik serta token akses (disimpan di penyimpanan kredensial sistem operasi), video dan caption yang dipilih pemilik (disimpan lokal dan hanya dikirim ke TikTok saat pemilik menjadwalkan unggahan ke draft miliknya sendiri), serta catatan lokal jadwal dan hasil unggahan. Data tidak dijual, tidak dibagikan ke pihak ketiga, dan tidak dipakai untuk iklan atau analitik. Pemilik dapat mencabut akses di pengaturan TikTok dan menghapus data lokal kapan saja.
